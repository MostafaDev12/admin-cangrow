About MWM Gulddal Systems

Founded in 2008 in Copenhagen's Gulddal Industrial Park, MWM Gulddal Systems has grown from a small engineering consultancy to Denmark's leading automation solutions provider, serving Fortune 500 companies across Northern Europe.

Our team of 45+ certified engineers, including 12 TÜV Functional Safety Engineers, specializes in complex automation projects for pharmaceutical manufacturing, renewable energy infrastructure, and food processing facilities requiring the highest safety and quality standards.

With headquarters in Copenhagen and regional offices in Malmö and Hamburg, we've successfully delivered over 300 automation projects, from small machine upgrades to complete greenfield facility automation systems.

MWM Gulddal Systems is also a leading worldwide supplier of industrial protective coatings, safety materials, and road markings. We are focused on developing sustainable coatings – our vision is to become the most sustainable industrial coatings company in the world. Our expertise is based on our long history. Today, our organization is global, ensuring fast service to customers from 18 locations in 12 countries with around 960 employees worldwide.
